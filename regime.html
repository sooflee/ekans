How to read this. A "strong fit" signal is one whose mechanism aligns with current conditions: golden cross is a trend-following rule, so it fits a trending market; VIX-term-structure is a contango/regime rule, so it fits a low-vol environment. A "crossing" signal is firing but the mechanism is fighting the regime — for example, mean-reversion signals during strong trends, or growth-cycle signals during contraction.

Fits are not backtest evidence under this regime. They are categorical reasoning: "this type of signal usually does X in conditions Y." Many signals haven't been tested across enough regimes to know for certain. Use this view to scale conviction — full size on aligned signals, half-size on neutral, quarter-size or skip on crossing.
