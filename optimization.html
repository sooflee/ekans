Optimized Baskets

Three optimization strategies applied to currently active signals. Click a card to see its detailed weights.

Not investment advice. Assumes signals are uncorrelated — a known weak assumption since many trend-following signals co-move. Volatility is implied (σ ≈ CAGR/Sharpe), not measured. Real correlations and live performance will differ.

Filter: Sharpe ≥ 0.5, CAGR > 0. Caps: 20% per signal (redistributed up to 5 passes). Strategies: Max Sharpe tangency under diagonal cov (w ∝ Sharpe²/CAGR); Max CAGR return-weighted (w ∝ CAGR); Risk Parity inverse-vol (w ∝ 1/σ) — each signal contributes equal risk.
