Optimized Basket — Max Sharpe

Mean-variance optimization over currently active signals. Reads live from the Signals tab — flip a signal active/defensive there and this updates.

Not investment advice. Assumes signals are uncorrelated — a known weak assumption since many trend-following signals co-move and the diagonal-covariance shortcut will overweight them. Volatility is implied (σ ≈ CAGR/Sharpe), not measured. Real correlations and live performance will differ.

Formula: wi ∝ Sharpei2/CAGRi — closed-form tangency portfolio under diagonal covariance. Filter: Sharpe ≥ 0.5, CAGR > 0. Caps: 20% per signal, redistributed up to 5 passes. Equal-weight baseline shown for reference.
